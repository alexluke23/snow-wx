    Privacy

    Snow-Wx sets no cookies, has no accounts, and runs no analytics or advertising today. This page lists exactly what the site does with your browser, so you can check it. It describes the site as it is built now and will be updated before anything changes.

    What we keep · What stays in your browser · Other companies your browser contacts · Links to other sites · If this changes

    What we keep about you

    • Nothing that identifies you. The site is a set of static files: there is no login, no form and no database that holds visitors.
    • The site is served from GitHub Pages (planned: Cloudflare). Like any web host, the host sees your IP address and the pages you request and keeps ordinary server logs under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use those logs. [OWNER: update when the host changes.]
    • If you click "Send feedback" or "Report a bust", your e-mail program opens with a message to the feedback address. Nothing is sent until you send it; if you do, we receive your e-mail address and what you wrote.

    What stays in your browser

    No cookies. The site stores a few settings in your browser's own storage, on your device only. They are never sent to us. Clear them any time with your browser's "clear site data".

    When the optional offline app mode is switched on (not yet), your browser also keeps a copy of the pages and your favorites' forecasts in its cache storage so they work without a connection.

    Other companies your browser contacts

    To draw maps and charts, your browser itself asks the services below for files. Each one therefore sees your IP address, your browser type and what you ask for (for map tiles, roughly the area you are looking at). We do not control their logs; see their privacy policies. Nothing else is requested from other sites when a page loads.

    No external fonts, trackers, advertising, social-media buttons or embedded videos are used. Webcams are links only: no webcam picture is loaded here.

    Links to other sites

    Links to the National Weather Service, avalanche centers, resorts, road-condition sites and others open their own websites, which have their own privacy practices. We add no tracking to those links and they open with rel="noopener noreferrer", so the destination is not told which page you came from.

    If this changes

    • Analytics: if we add visitor counts they will be cookieless, with no personal data, no fingerprinting, and they will honour "Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control. Until then none is loaded (the code is switched off and makes no request).
    • E-mail alerts (planned) would need your e-mail address; we would ask you to confirm it, send you a one-click unsubscribe link and delete it when you unsubscribe. This page will say so before launch.
    • Children: the site is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
