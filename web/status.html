    When each data source last updated successfully, how old it is, and whether it is inside the limit the site monitors (the same limits that open a data-health issue for the owner). This page reads one small file, data/meta.json, written by every update. No cookies, no tracking.

    How to read this

    • OK: updated within its limit. Late: older than its limit or failing repeatedly: the data on the site may be out of date; the cell says since when. Not reported: no status yet (for example right after a deploy).
    • SNOTEL is fetched from the USDA NRCS service. When that service is down the site keeps the last good copy for up to 48 hours, labelled with its age, and then shows "unavailable"; it never shows old values as current.
    • NBM point forecasts update every 6 hours, NWS (NDFD) and model maps every 3 hours; observations, alerts and hazards hourly (GitHub's schedules are best-effort and can run late).

    Something looks wrong? Tell us. Safety information (avalanche danger, NWS alerts) always links to its official source: check weather.gov and your avalanche center.
