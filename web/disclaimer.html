    Disclaimer

    Snow-Wx is not for avalanche, travel or life-safety decisions. It shows weather-model guidance and observations. Mountain weather and snow conditions can change quickly and models are often wrong at a single point on a mountain.

    Avalanche safety

    • Avalanche danger shown here is the rating published by your local avalanche center, copied unaltered and greyed out when it has expired. We never make our own rating. Read the center's full forecast every time, and go to avalanche.org to find your center.
    • Backcountry travel needs training, the right equipment and a partner who knows how to use it. A forecast on this site does not replace any of that.
    • Labels such as "wind-affected snow likely", "powder quality" and "model agreement" are heuristics we wrote. They are not accuracy claims and not avalanche ratings.

    Weather

    • This is not an official forecast. For warnings and official forecasts use the National Weather Service.
    • Point forecasts come from coarse model grids (about 2.5 km for NBM, 28 km for ECMWF). We adjust for height in a simple way; a ridge, a bowl or a road can differ a lot.
    • Snow depth, temperature and wind observations are automatic and provisional. They can be wrong, missing or out of date; the page says when they are.
    • Data can arrive late or stop. The Data status page shows what is current.

    Roads, parking and permits

    Road, parking, Sno-Park permit and webcam information is linked from the agencies and resorts that publish it. Check their sites before you travel. Links to other sites are outside our control.
