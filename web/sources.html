    Data sources and credits

    Every number on Snow-Wx comes from a public source. This page lists each one, the licence it comes under and the credit it asks for. We are not affiliated with any of them, and none of them endorses this site.

    Weather, snow and avalanche data

    NOAA / National Weather Service

    National Blend of Models (NBM) point forecasts, alerts, forecast discussions, winter hazards; links to the full NWS forecast; the NDFD forecast is fetched only for the verification archive.

    Licence: US Government work: public domain · terms

    Credit: Data: NOAA / National Weather Service

    NOAA / NWS NCEP Global Ensemble Forecast System (GEFS)

    The NOAA GEFS ensemble plume (Ensemble view, ECMWF ENS | NOAA GEFS switch): total precipitation and a snow estimate at ski-area summits, all members.

    Licence: US Government work: public domain · terms

    Credit: Data: NOAA / NWS National Centers for Environmental Prediction (NCEP) GEFS, via the NOAA Open Data Dissemination program

    NOAA / National Weather Service winter-hazard map service

    The shaded winter-weather warning, watch and advisory areas on the obs map.

    Licence: US Government work: public domain · terms

    Credit: Hazard areas: NOAA / National Weather Service

    NOAA National Operational Hydrologic Remote Sensing Center (NOHRSC) and SNODAS

    Snowfall analysis and SNODAS snow depth overlays and storm totals.

    Licence: US Government work: public domain · terms

    Credit: Snow analysis: NOAA NOHRSC (SNODAS via NSIDC)

    USDA NRCS SNOTEL (AWDB)

    Snow depth, snow water equivalent and temperature at 800+ SNOTEL stations.

    Licence: US Government work: public domain · terms

    Credit: SNOTEL data: USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service, National Water and Climate Center. Provisional data, subject to revision.

    Iowa Environmental Mesonet (IEM)

    Licence: Free for any lawful use, including commercial (IEM's stated terms) · terms

    Credit: Observations: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University

    avalanche.org and the regional avalanche centers (terms being confirmed before launch)

    Data we publish

    The ski-area and Sno-Park catalogs (data/ski_areas.json, data/rec_points.json) are derived from OpenStreetMap and OpenSkiMap and are therefore made available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL): you may use and adapt them if you credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" and share derived databases under the same licence. Forecast numbers shown on pages are derived from NOAA/NWS and ECMWF data (see above) and carry no additional claim.

    ECMWF data are adapted: we read point values from ECMWF's gridded files, accumulate precipitation, and compute percentiles across ensemble members. ECMWF has not endorsed these adaptations.
